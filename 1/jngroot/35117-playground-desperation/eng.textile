Playground desperation

Author: jngroot

*000*2017-01-31 19:20

I don't get to see true desperation very often. Last weekend I was lucky.

My neighbourhood has a small playground that I sometimes visit with my child. It has a few swings, a nice slide and some climbing frames. The playground is quite popular, especially on sunny days. Usually I bring something to read and sit on a bench while my child enjoys himself, either alone or with other children.

I had just arrived when a mother with 2 children walked by. Appareantly they just had been to the supermarket, as she was carrying a grocery bag. She was actually quite good-looking: a curvy body and curly brown hair. She was wearing fairly tight jeans. I estimated her age as late twenties.

The children (age 6 and 8, I would estimate) saw the playground and immediately went off to the swings. The mother was a bit annoyed: she said that she wanted to go home. Then she gave in and said: OK, 5 minutes and then we dash home. Her daugher asked, why so soon already? And then she said: well, mommys also have to pee sometimes.

She then said politely hello to me and sat next to me on the bench to watch her children play. I pretended to read but actually I was quite curious if and how events would unfold. I immediately noticed that she had all the signs of serious desperation: she was very fidgety, legs crossed, could n't sit still. I did n't really know what to do, but I decided to start some small talk with reference to the situation. I said that the playground was very nice, the only thing it missed was a bathroom.

She grinned and said something like she should drink less coffee before going out shopping. Then she said: god, I'm really bursting, I think I really should head home now with the kids. She stood up and walked to her kids in order to go home. Of course, they started to protest but she said, please.. mommy is about to pee herself so for this time, be co-operative. The kids probably realised that it was serious.

I jokingly said: I hope you make it home in time.

She said: I really do hope so.. God I have to go soo bad.

Then she started to walk firmly away from the playground and her children followed. Another detail I noticed is that she never held her hand between her legs (that would also be kind of undignified) but she did put her hand on her belly a few times, as if that was were the pressure was building up.

The episode did n't last very long, no wetting involved, but at least it was a classic case of true public desperation
