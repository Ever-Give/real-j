Sighting at work

Author: Jenna

*000*2016-06-17 21:20

I have lurked here for a while, but I had an experience a few days ago that I want to share.

My college hires students at the end of the quarter to help clean out the dorms, which will be used as hotels during various camps and conferences that are held during the off season. Everything has to be cleaned out in one week. The supervisors are all sophomores and juniors in their first supervisory position and so many of them enforce rules to the letter. One of these rules is minimizing bathroom breaks outside of break times. During breaks coffee and water are provided, and water is always available at the worksite. I was waxing the floor in the hallway and a girl from another crew was nearby sweeping and mopping the stairs. She was fairly attractive and I thought, trying for a 50s aesthetic--a pink bandana around her hair, cut off overalls, and her hair in a braid. She was short, a little plump, had brown hair and glasses. At one point one of the team leads, one of the nicer ones, came by and reminded us to take water breaks since it was a hot day.

'Oh thanks I already had plenty to drink,' she said. I wasn't really interested until I saw her sitting on her heel as she knelt down to scrape some gum off the stairs. Our break came around I went with the others to get some snacks and coffee. I passed her outside the bathrooms with a cup of something (I don't know if it was coffee or water) talking on her phone. I guess she had intended to go, but got stuck in a call. I hard her reciting a zip code so I guess it was something important enough that she waited to pee when she seemed uncomfortable already.

We were back in the same spot cleaning (I was washing windows now) and after about 20 minutes she was finished prepping the stairs and came back with water and a mop. It must have been torture hearing the water dripping because she was now shifting her weight constantly and crossed her legs a couple of times. She had been chatting with me earlier and now she was much quieter. At one point she asked if her team lead, who was strict, was still outside pressure washing.

"Yeah, she's down there. Why?"

"Oh, just wondering." She tried to sound casual but she seemed nervous.

It wasn't until I was downstairs cleaning the door, almost 40 minutes later, that I saw the girl from earlier come rushing out. The team lead noticed her heading away from the building.

"Where are you going?" she asked.

''Where's the nearest bathroom?" The ones in the break area were about 5 minutes away.

''In the ground floor of the first dorm.'' This was across the courtyard. ''But you should--''

''I need to pee now,'' the girl said, hurrying off.

I thought it would have been creepy to follow her. But later on I noted a small wet patch between her legs--she was squatting down and it wasn't visible when she was standing or sitting normally. She must have had to go quite badly! Our lunch ends at 1 and our next break was at 4, when she was already desperate enough to sit on her heel, but drank another 12 ounce cup of liquid (assuming it was full, but still). Then held on for the extra hour, hearing the dripping water constantly from the mop bucket. I imagine it must have been a desperate few moments undoing her overalls.
