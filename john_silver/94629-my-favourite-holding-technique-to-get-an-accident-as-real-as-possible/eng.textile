What is a perfect hold? I guess everyone has their own preferences, so what I am presenting is by no means an ultimate truth, rather something that has been working for me.

First of all, what I would like to experience, is a "real accident". Quite often, when holding, I just become kinda too uncomfortable, and start releasing half-voluntarily. What I am aiming at, is a struggle to the end, and a totally involuntary wetting.

Second, my thing(TM) is "pee shyness", or "too shy to ask", or "I am holding and trying not to show it because showing my need to pee is embarassing and childish".

The conclusion that I have come to is this

1) The goal of this hold is to role-play to not to have an accident, but to avoid it. And,

2) What regulates your hold is time and shyness.

The setting is a day in high school. Remember those days you longingly watched the clock on the wall, and when it moved so slowly? How you pedaled sitting on your school chair? Had an accident on the bus on your way home? This hold is designed to role-play those days again.

Shyness and shyness check

Shyness is a factor. As in role playing games, this is your leading stat (and only stat), ranging from 0 (no shyness) to 100 (absolute shyness). How it works: when you will consider either using the toilet (during breaks) or asking for a permission to go to the toilet (during classes), you must roll a d100 against your shyness. For example: you have decided (or the rules so dictate that you must) to ask for a permission during class, you roll d100 and if your roll is higher or equal to your shyness, you will have succeeded in your shyness check and you'll proceed to ask the permission. I suggest starting at a shyness level of 85, this has given me some good results.

Cooldown

Every time you roll a shyness check, you will start a 10 min cooldown, after which the check can be rolled again. No exceptions.

Asking for permission / going to toilet, when to ask

You have some liberty on asking for permission (during class) or just going (breaks). You can ask the permission, if your need is at a level where you have significant discomfort and cannot stay still (on numbers at least 7-8/10). You MUST ask if you have leaked involultarily, or are in severe pain (9-10/10). If you are on cooldown, you cannot ask nor go to the toilet in any condition.

Class

If you succeed in your shyness check, you'll have to roll an additional roll on the d100 and score at least 50 to gain permission. If you gain permission, go to the toilet and void completely. Failure results in a 10 min cooldown, after which you may roll again, altough this time you'll have to succeed in two (2) shyness checks to muster your courage for asking the permission. Or you can wait 20 min and then things fall back to normal, requiring just one check. It's up to you. 😉

When in class (or bus) you must:

- sit on your chair (preferably hard wooden or plastic), next to a table

- do whatever with your computer, book, etc.

You must not:

- stand up or sit on the floor - eception is a brief rise from the chair to "straigten your skirt" or whatever 😉

You can:

- bend and rock on the chair, pedal, hold yourself etc. as long as you keep it as discreet as possible

- clean any puddles on the floor if they occur, this challenge is not ment to ruin your flooring

Breaks

If you succeed in your shyness check, you'll have to roll an additional roll on the d100 to check whether the toilet has bullies (roll under 50, it has them). If it has, you must first succeed in a shyness check to go there, and then roll d100 and get at least 50, to the bullies to let you in. Failure results in a normal 10 min cooldown after which you can roll again.

When on breaks you must:

- stand or walk around

You must not:

- sit anywhere

You can:

- do a pee-dance and all whatever you need to hold, as long as you do not sit or lay down, but try to keep it discreet

Schedule

The daily routine is the following (you can shift the times of course, to start later than 9 am):

9:00 - home - drink a large cup of coffee and 1 l of water/soda and have your morning pee (void completely).

9:15-10:00 - bus- Bus has no toilets. Sit on your chair and take your time.

10:00-10:45 - class - 1st class

10:45-11:00 - break - 1st break

11:00-11:45 - class - 2nd class

11:45-12:00 - break - lunch break - drink a can of energy drink and 1 l of water (if you are in severe need, just 0.5 l, at your choice)

12:00-12:45 - class - 3rd class

12:45-13:00 - break - 3rd break - drink a can of energy drink (if your need is very uncomfortable, can skip)

13:00-13:45 - class - 4th class

13:45-14:00 - bus stop - wait 15 standing for the bus - drink a can of energy drink (can skip as before)

14:00-15:45 - bus - bus ride home, no toilets as before

15:45-16:00 - walk - 15 min walk home

Front door

When the daily schedule is complete, there is one final task of opening your front door and rushing to the toilet. To open your front door lock, you must have prepared beforehands:

Buy 4 similar padlocks (2 keys each) and a keyring. Discard 3 of the locks and the other of the keys to the remaining lock. Insert the 7 keys to an keyring. You should have one lock and a keyring with 7 similar-looking keys, only one of which opens the lock.

To succesfully open your front door, you must succeed in opening the padlock. The lock should be attached to something. If you don't have anything else, keep it hanging on your toilet door handle, or some other similar place.

When you have opened the lock, you are permitted to go to the toilet, take off your clothes and void completely.

Scoring

The goal is not to have an accident. Hence:

Major success = go through the whole day without using the toilet at school nor having accidents

Success = go through the whole day without having accidents

Minor success = go through the whole day with small leaks that do not show much (you are the judge)

Minor loss = a small accident that is not clearly visible (again, you are the judge)

Loss = a large accident on the bus / home that is clearly visble

Major loss = a large accident in school that is clearly visible

Failure = quit the challenge before the day was over

Additional notes

The liquid intake is kept to a moderate degree. The goal is to have a slow build-up of desperation, that nonetheless raises to critical levels. I am aware that some of you are capable on insane holds, so adjust at your will. What I wrote works for me, however. Especially the energy drinks are... oh my.

If you wet yourself, the hold is not over. You must struggle for the whole day. Nor it is over if you manage to use the toilet through shyness checks. The liquid intake is designed so that it will result in getting desperate again.

In my experience, why I have liked this hold is a) it is a role-play, with its rules and restrictions, and b) salvation is always theoretically one cooldown away, which makes you hold just that little bit longer. Except the bus ride home of course, but that is the "endgame", so there is salvation, but only the wait is longer, hence the logic is the same. The hard time caps you know beforehands are the core ingredient here, as well as role-playing for avoiding wetting (however sweet that uncontrollable wetting is)!

What you wear is up to you. I personally am a huge fan of skirts, tights and overalls, but it is your pick. Accidents should show, however.
